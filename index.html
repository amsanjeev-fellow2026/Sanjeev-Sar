Maths

Operations, revision, quiz-show challenges, angles, number sense and Roman numerals.

Team game

Zor Lagake Maths

A tug of war for the four operations.

Two teams, one rope, and a separate question for each side. Pick the operations and difficulty, name your teams and shout a chant before the countdown. A right answer pulls the rope your way. A wrong answer hands the point to the other team. Get ahead by six to win.

  • Addition, subtraction, multiplication, division
  • Easy, medium, hard
  • Two teams
  • 5 music tracks
Play Zor Lagake Maths
Team game

Dream Team Penalty Shootout

A penalty shootout for maths revision.

Teams take turns choosing a spot in the goal. Behind every square is a question, and the harder squares are worth more points. A right answer scores a goal. A wrong answer is saved or goes off target. Hidden cards, lifelines, music and crowd sounds keep the whole class cheering.

  • Numbers, measurement, time, mixed
  • Easy, medium, hard
  • 2 to 4 teams
  • 4 music tracks
  • Rules page and lifelines
Play Dream Team Penalty Shootout
Solo game

Who Wants to Be a Math Champion?

A quiz-show climb through 15 questions.

One player takes the hot seat and climbs five levels, from easy Rookie questions to tough Champion word problems. Finish a level to earn a badge and unlock a reward: bonus time, double points, refilled lifelines, and unlimited time in the final level. Stuck? Use 50:50, Ask a Friend or Flip the Question.

  • 15 questions, 5 levels
  • Numbers, patterns, measurement, time, geometry, data
  • A reward after every level
  • 3 lifelines
  • Timer on or off
Play Who Wants to Be a Math Champion?
Team game

Maths Penalty Cup

A penalty shootout with a question for every team.

Set up two to five teams, give each its own level and paste in the student names. Every round each team gets its own question and a shooter steps up. A right answer scores a goal, a wrong one is saved or goes wide, and three goals in a row make every goal after that worth double. Fair play mode gives everyone an equal turn, and the top scorer wins the Golden Boot.

  • 2 to 5 teams
  • 4 levels, Rookie to Legend
  • 12 maths topics to choose from
  • Timer or chill mode
  • Saved classes
Play Maths Penalty Cup

Roman numerals

Two games: first break the code, then discover the rules.

Part 1

The King's Secret Code

A story, a king and a target number.

Help an explorer reach the king's number using three magical stones worth 1, 5 and 10 steps. Each stone has limited uses, so students plan before they jump. Land on the number exactly and the ancient symbols I, V and X are revealed.

  • Story intro
  • Symbols I, V, X
  • Planning and number sense
Play The King's Secret Code
Part 2

Roman Quest

Discover the rules by moving.

Move along a number road with the I, V and X stones and watch the Roman numeral build as you go. Along the way students find the rules for adding, repeating and subtracting, and collect them in a Rule Book. Earn coins and stars, or roll the dice for a new target.

  • 14 rules to discover
  • Coins and stars
  • Rule Book
Play Roman Quest

Angles

Two ways to measure, add and name angles.

Solo game

Slice Rush

Fill the tray with exactly the right slices.

Customers order trays of exactly 90° or 180°. Tap angle slices to fill the tray with no gap and no overflow. Some customers ask for a particular kind of slice, such as an acute or an obtuse one. Every served order shows the angle sum, and you have three lives to get through ten orders.

  • Acute, right, obtuse, straight
  • Corner tray 90° and straight tray 180°
  • 10 orders, 3 lives
  • Chill mode with no timer
  • Teacher controls
Play Slice Rush
Solo game

Angle Factory

Fix the factory with four angle games.

Aim the laser cannon at the right robot, bank a shot off the mirror, find the missing angle against the clock, or spot the point, line, line segment and ray. Each game runs ten rounds with three lives, and the last rounds go blind with no angle readout.

  • 4 game modes
  • Drag or tap to aim
  • Missing angles on 90° and 180°
  • Point, line, segment, ray
  • Teacher controls
Play Angle Factory

Five-frame number sense

Two games for seeing a number at a glance.

Solo or team game

Flash Frame

See it. Remember it. Know it!

A five-frame flashes on screen for a fraction of a second. Remember how many dots you saw, then tap the number. Four levels shrink the flash from half a second to a tenth, and varied dot patterns arrive at the top level. Play alone or race two or three teams.

  • 4 levels
  • 0.5 to 0.1 second flash
  • Numbers 1 to 5
  • Team race
  • Missed numbers for the teacher
Play Flash Frame
Class game

Frame Detective

One glance. One mystery.

A five-frame appears and vanishes. Detectives solve one of four mysteries: how many dots, what changed, what is missing, or a memory match. Choose Rookie to Master, with the frame showing for 3 seconds down to 0.75 seconds. Play as a class or in up to four teams.

  • 4 kinds of mystery
  • Rookie to Master
  • Up to 4 teams
  • Hints and retry
  • Teacher setup
Play Frame Detective

Reading Fluency

Read aloud, blend sounds and build confidence, one word at a time.

Reading Fluency games

Two ways to practise the same RF progressions.

Solo game

RF Word Finder

Find it. Read it. Score it!

Hunt for hidden words in a letter grid, then read each one aloud while the teacher confirms. Pick a reading pattern such as short vowels, blends or digraphs, or switch to a text hunt that finds phrases, sentences or letter patterns in a short passage. Hints and retries help, and no points are ever taken away.

  • 10 RF progressions
  • Easy, standard, challenge
  • Tap or drag to select
  • Running record for the teacher
Play RF Word Finder
Solo game

Dream Team Word Striker

Reading fluency, one shot at a time.

A penalty shootout for reading aloud. The student reads a word, phrase, sentence or passage, the teacher says how it went, and the ball flies in: a goal for a clean read or self-correction, an assist after a prompt, and a training assist when the word needs modelling. Harder words arrive as the match moves from Warm-up to Championship.

  • 9 RF progressions
  • Supported, practice, challenge
  • 5, 10 or 15 shots
  • 3 music tracks
  • Running record for the teacher
Play Dream Team Word Striker

Science

Food, spoilage, preservation and healthy living.

Class game

BioCity

Build a healthy world, one district at a time.

Students run a growing town and make science decisions in four districts: Nutrition, Food Security, Well-being and Safety. Missions include stocking a market, building a balanced meal, choosing how to preserve food and inspecting a kitchen. Finish a district to earn its badge, then take the Healthy City Certification.

  • 4 districts and a final certification
  • 10, 20 or 30 minute sessions
  • Whole class, teams or individual
  • Smart-board mode
  • Teacher panel with review items
Play BioCity
Mission game

Food Forensics

Crack the case of the spoiled food.

Open a case file and work through four rooms. Inspect spoiled samples, match clues to the factors that spoil food, choose the right preservation method, then crack the final code to open the vault. Every answer comes with an explanation, and each mission earns a stamp.

  • 4 missions
  • Signs of spoilage, causes, preservation
  • Explanation for every answer
  • A stamp for each mission
  • Case report
Play Food Forensics
Solo game

The BMI Experience

Measure like a health scientist.

Pick a character, then measure like a scientist. In the Height Lab choose the right tool, strike the right pose and read the ruler. In the Weight Lab steady the scale and read the dial. In the BMI Lab build the formula and work out the answer. Hints are there when needed, and the game ends with a Health Scientist certificate.

  • 3 labs
  • 6 characters
  • 7 steps, 1 star each
  • Hints
  • Certificate out of 100
Play The BMI Experience
Solo game

Feed Pip

Feed Pip, move Pip, and watch the choices show.

Pick from 16 foods and watch Pip eat them. Healthy meals keep Pip light and happy, while too much fast food makes Pip heavy, tired and out of breath. Then let Pip run, play football, skip rope or cycle, and see what a mobile game does instead. A live BMI bar and an energy see-saw show what is happening, and a 5-day mission ends with a star report.

  • 16 foods and 5 activities
  • Pip eats, runs, kicks, skips and cycles
  • Live BMI bar and energy see-saw
  • A fact after every choice
  • 5-day mission with a star report
Play Feed Pip

Class Culture

For any subject: team points, rewards and memory games that build a class that plays together.

Teacher tool

Class Culture Hub

A points board that turns good days into games.

Three teams collect points all day, and the board shows who is leading, each team's level from Rookie to Champion, and how far they are from the next unlock. Enter the points at the end of the day and track daily streaks. Games unlock as teams reach their targets, and a random student picker calls the next players up.

  • 3 teams
  • Levels from Rookie to Champion
  • End-of-day points and streaks
  • Games that unlock at set points
  • Random student picker
  • Teacher PIN and backup
Open Class Culture Hub
Team game

Class Cup

Matchday for the whole class: every student gets a kick.

Add the teams, paste in the student names and kick off. Choose a penalty kick, a free kick over the wall or a target blitz with no keeper. Every team gets the same number of kicks, so smaller teams simply kick more often. An absent student can be swapped for a teammate, and a saved match can be resumed later.

  • Penalty kick, free kick, target blitz
  • Every student gets a kick
  • Fair turns for every team
  • Absent? Call a teammate
  • Resume a saved match
  • Sound on or off
Play Class Cup
Team game

What Was Said?

Six levels, six memory games.

Teams take turns at six memory challenges, and clearing a level unlocks the next: Story Recall, Emoji Flip, Word Hunt, Memory Blocks, What Changed? and a final Number Boss. Pick the difficulty, choose which games to include and turn on double points for the last level. If a student is absent, call another teammate.

  • 6 levels, each a different memory game
  • Easy, normal, hard
  • Teams and student names
  • Double points on the last level
  • Teacher settings
  • Background music
Play What Was Said?
