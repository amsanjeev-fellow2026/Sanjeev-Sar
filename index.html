Maths

Operations, revision, quiz-show challenges and Roman numerals.

Team game

Zor Lagake Maths

A tug of war for the four operations.

Two teams, one rope, and a separate question for each side. Pick the operations and difficulty, name your teams and shout a chant before the countdown. A right answer pulls the rope your way. A wrong answer hands the point to the other team. Get ahead by six to win.

  • Addition, subtraction, multiplication, division
  • Easy, medium, hard
  • Two teams
  • 5 music tracks

Team game

Dream Team Penalty Shootout

A penalty shootout for maths revision.

Teams take turns choosing a spot in the goal. Behind every square is a question, and the harder squares are worth more points. A right answer scores a goal. A wrong answer is saved or goes off target. Hidden cards, lifelines, music and crowd sounds keep the whole class cheering.

  • Numbers, measurement, time, mixed
  • Easy, medium, hard
  • 2 to 4 teams
  • 4 music tracks
  • Rules page and lifelines
Play Dream Team Penalty Shootout
Solo game

Who Wants to Be a Math Champion?

A quiz-show climb through 15 questions.

One player takes the hot seat and climbs five levels, from easy Rookie questions to tough Champion word problems. Finish a level to earn a badge and unlock a reward: bonus time, double points, refilled lifelines, and unlimited time in the final level. Stuck? Use 50:50, Ask a Friend or Flip the Question.

  • 15 questions, 5 levels
  • Numbers, patterns, measurement, time, geometry, data
  • A reward after every level
  • 3 lifelines
  • Timer on or off
Play Who Wants to Be a Math Champion?

Roman numerals

Two games, best played in this order.

Part 1

The King's Secret Code

A story, a king and a target number.

Help an explorer reach the king's number using three magical stones worth 1, 5 and 10 steps. Each stone has limited uses, so students plan before they jump. Land on the number exactly and the ancient symbols I, V and X are revealed.

  • Story intro
  • Symbols I, V, X
  • Planning and number sense
Play The King's Secret Code
Part 2

Roman Quest

Discover the rules by moving.

Move along a number road with the I, V and X stones and watch the Roman numeral build as you go. Along the way students find the rules for adding, repeating and subtracting, and collect them in a Rule Book. Earn coins and stars, or roll the dice for a new target.

  • 14 rules to discover
  • Coins and stars
  • Rule Book
Play Roman Quest

Reading Fluency

Read aloud, blend sounds and build confidence, one word at a time.

Reading Fluency games

Two ways to practise the same RF progressions.

Solo game

RF Word Finder

Find it. Read it. Score it!

Hunt for hidden words in a letter grid, then read each one aloud while the teacher confirms. Pick a reading pattern such as short vowels, blends or digraphs, or switch to a text hunt that finds phrases, sentences or letter patterns in a short passage. Hints and retries help, and no points are ever taken away.

  • 10 RF progressions
  • Easy, standard, challenge
  • Tap or drag to select
  • Running record for the teacher
Play RF Word Finder
Solo game

Dream Team Word Striker

Reading fluency, one shot at a time.

A penalty shootout for reading aloud. The student reads a word, phrase, sentence or passage, the teacher says how it went, and the ball flies in: a goal for a clean read or self-correction, an assist after a prompt, and a training assist when the word needs modelling. Harder words arrive as the match moves from Warm-up to Championship.

  • 9 RF progressions
  • Supported, practice, challenge
  • 5, 10 or 15 shots
  • 3 music tracks
  • Running record for the teacher
Play Dream Team Word Striker
